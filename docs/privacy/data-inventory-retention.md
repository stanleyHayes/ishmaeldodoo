## Data minimisation rules

1. Collect a field only when a documented workflow uses it; optional free text requires a named purpose.
2. Public forms never send personal or confidential values to analytics, session replay or client error reporting.
3. Logs use internal correlation identifiers and bounded error categories, never bodies, tokens, email addresses, Room references or enquiry content.
4. Public projections use explicit allowlists and exclude drafts, internal notes, consent evidence and operational metadata.
5. Provider adapters receive only the fields necessary for the single delivery operation.
6. Production support access is role-bound, time-bound and audited; engineers cannot use database exports as support tooling.

## Subject rights workflow

- Privacy owner acknowledges an access, correction, objection, restriction or erasure request and records a non-sensitive case reference.
- Identity is verified proportionately without copying unnecessary identity documents into product collections.
- The owner searches every applicable system and processor, including queues, Cloudinary and restored/exported data, and responds within the brief's 30-day service level.
- Erasure removes or pseudonymises personal fields without falsifying immutable state history. Hash-chain continuity alone is not a reason to retain readable personal data.
- Any refusal or retention exception requires a Legal-approved reason category and appeal/escalation route; do not store free-text legal analysis in product audit events.
- Processor actions and backup reconciliation are included before closure evidence is signed.

## DPIA screening

The processing requires a formal DPIA before production because it combines a serving public official's institutional network, travel/engagement information, confidential Room content, systematic triage, public scholar stories and cross-border processors. Plausible harms include political/reputational exposure, physical-security risk, financial/procurement abuse, discrimination through profiling, loss of confidentiality, account takeover and inability to honour deletion.

Controls already present include data-boundary separation, role checks, MFA foundations, ciphertext-only Room design, redacted logs, publication governance, consent fields, provider adapters and finite TTLs for several request classes. A synthetic stale Room snapshot is reconciled from separately preserved content-free deletion evidence before access, and the four TTL-backed requester collections are independently purged to the restore cutoff. Residual high risks remain until Room cryptography/key custody is independently reviewed, database users and provider backups are isolated, provider regions/contracts are approved, production subject-rights and deletion/restore drills pass, WAF/monitoring are deployed, and penetration testing closes high/critical findings.

## Approval and implementation gaps

Legal/Data Protection must approve each lawful basis, privacy notice, processor/subprocessor, international transfer mechanism, retention duration, legal-hold rule, subject-rights identity procedure and audit pseudonymisation approach. Security/Infrastructure must provide collection-level access tests, encryption/key custody, region and backup lifecycle evidence. Product owners must remove any field without a demonstrated workflow purpose.

Engineering has encoded the brief-defined Protocol Desk schedule in migration 026 and the separate retention command, including restore reconciliation and adversarial replica tests. The Room enforces its primary 180-day schedule; protected aggregate monitoring and sustained alerts cover failed scans, failed deletions and work more than 75 minutes overdue without identifying an enquiry, and the recovery harness proves synthetic stale-snapshot reconciliation from independent content-free deletion evidence. General contact, media enquiry, Press Kit and Living Dossier TTL paths expose fixed-class aggregate due/age health every five minutes, page after two hours without selecting requester fields, and are purged to an explicit cutoff during authenticated stale-snapshot restoration before access. Governed media expiry now shares the isolated retention command and performs publication-safe Cloudinary destruction with quarantine and retry. Remaining unapproved schedules and provider execution evidence remain. AMANOR-113 remains open until the signed DPIA and complete schedule exist and all controls are verified in a production-like environment.
