Use only synthetic, non-identifying records and provider objects. Evidence may
contain fixed-class aggregate counts, timestamps, release identifiers, alert or
incident identifiers, provider audit-event identifiers and durable HTTPS links.
It must not contain requester fields, Room ciphertext or plaintext, media
contents, credentials, connection strings, keys, tokens or copied provider
logs. Automated repository tests and a local restore prove engineering
behavior; they do not replace this production-like provider exercise or its
three independent approvals.
